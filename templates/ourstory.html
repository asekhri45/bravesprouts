Behind MyBraveSprout

Our Story

MyBraveSprout grew out of a personal family experience and has continued to grow through conversations with families and specialists.

It was created to give children a more comfortable place to practice speaking, with gentle opportunities to build confidence from home.

  • Free for families
  • Shaped with specialists
  • Practice at home, at your child's pace

Personal Beginning

Where MyBraveSprout began

MyBraveSprout grew out of my family’s experience with selective mutism.

My younger sister was talkative and playful at home, but speaking in many settings outside our home was much harder for her. As my family learned how to support her, I began to understand how anxiety could make speaking feel incredibly difficult, even when a child wanted to communicate.

Over time, she became more comfortable speaking in situations that had once felt overwhelming. Years later, she was elected president of her school.

Her progress took time, patience, and many opportunities to practice in settings where she felt comfortable. Watching that process showed me how important it was for support to continue beyond scheduled sessions and become part of everyday life.

As I became more involved in computer science and software development, I began wondering whether I could build something that made those lower-pressure practice opportunities easier to access at home.

Seeing the confident and expressive person our family had always known begin to share that part of herself more freely with the people around her became my greatest inspiration for creating MyBraveSprout. I wanted to build something that could help other children and families take similar steps.

The purpose

A comfortable place to practice

MyBraveSprout is a free, interactive platform designed to give children gentle opportunities to practice speaking from the comfort of home.

Through game-like activities and friendly animated characters, children can practice answering questions, making choices, asking for help, and participating in simple conversations. The activities begin with smaller, lower-pressure interactions and gradually encourage more independent responses.

The goal is not to pressure children into speaking. It is to help speaking feel a little safer, more familiar, and more achievable, one step at a time.

An additional resource

MyBraveSprout is not intended to replace professional care. It was created as an additional resource that families can use between sessions and alongside the strategies recommended by their child’s specialists.

Growing together

Built with families and specialists

Although MyBraveSprout began with my family’s story, it cannot be built from our experience alone.

I have met with selective mutism specialists and families to better understand what children need and how technology may support them responsibly. I hope to continue having these conversations as MyBraveSprout grows.

Parents and children have also helped shape the platform through their feedback. Their experiences have influenced the activities, characters, pacing, parent resources, and the way the platform responds to each child.

MyBraveSprout is still growing. Every conversation with a family or specialist teaches me something new, and every piece of feedback helps make the platform more thoughtful, accessible, and useful.
