How the activities gradually progress

Every activity follows the same four gentle stages, so your child always knows what to expect next.

1

Start with the star

With the help of a parent, children first interact with the MyBraveSprout star through simple games and playful activities. This stage focuses on helping the child feel comfortable, safe, and familiar with the experience.

2

Meet new friends

Once the child feels more at ease, the star introduces additional characters through interactive games and guided activities. Each new character gently expands the child's comfort zone while keeping the experience playful.

3

Practice conversations

After meeting the characters, children begin practicing short guided conversations. These scenarios are supportive and structured, helping kids respond and participate while building confidence step by step.

4

Speak with confidence

As confidence grows, conversations become more realistic and dynamic. Characters, voices, and situations gradually resemble real-life interactions, preparing children for everyday speaking moments.

Built from real experience, refined with real feedback

Why we built this

MyBraveSprout was inspired by the founder's younger sister's experience with selective mutism — and the belief that every child deserves safe, low-pressure opportunities to practice speaking.

Shaped with specialists and families

The app has been improved using feedback from selective mutism specialists, therapists, and the families who tested it early on. A licensed clinical psychologist specializing in childhood anxiety and selective mutism has provided feedback on activity design.

Parent-guided, not a replacement for care

Every activity is meant to be used alongside a parent, not instead of one. MyBraveSprout is an additional resource families can use between sessions — it is not intended to replace professional care.

Voice, AI, and your family's information

  • What MyBraveSprout stores: your parent account information, your child's in-app progress (like which activities are unlocked), and settings you choose — not audio recordings or word-for-word transcripts of what your child says during an activity.
  • What happens to your child's voice: during a speaking activity, your child's voice is converted to text so the app can respond, then that audio is discarded — it is not saved to MyBraveSprout's database.
  • Parent controls: a 4-digit parent PIN protects parent-only areas like settings, and you can permanently delete your account and its associated data at any time from Settings.

Frequently asked questions

Is MyBraveSprout really free?

Yes. Creating a parent account and using the activities is free.

What is "Limited Early Access"?

MyBraveSprout is intentionally welcoming a limited early group of families so we can closely monitor the experience, act on feedback quickly, and keep activity quality high as the platform grows.

What ages is this designed for?

MyBraveSprout is designed for children ages 8 and under with selective mutism.
